      Getting started
      
    
        
      
      1. Pick your tools. Set them once under My setup at the top. Every prompt and install step on this page adapts.
        2. 
      
      2. Install the skills. Follow Install. It takes about two minutes. In chat-only tools there is nothing to install.
        3. 
      
      3. Say where you are. Open Process, pick your phase and copy a prompt. If unsure, ask ux-process-guide.
        4. 
      
      4. Answer the skill's questions. A skill asks for what it needs. It will not invent users, quotes or requirements, so have your material ready.
        5. 
      
      5. Do your part. Every skill ends with a block called Your judgment. Those checks are the design work.
        6. 
      
      6. Save as you go. Keep outputs as files in your project and commit after each working step.
        7. 
    
      
  
      
  
      
    
      
      
      What a skill is
      
      
      A folder with a file called SKILL.md: plain-text instructions that an AI agent loads when your request matches. Open one and read it. That is part of the learning, and you can change it or write your own.
      

      your-project/
├── .agents/skills/      the toolkit skills
├── PRD.md               what you are building
├── DESIGN.md            how it looks and feels
├── AGENTS.md            how the agent should work
├── PROMPTS.md           your prompt log
└── research/            notes, themes, personas
      
    
      
    
      
      
      Ground rules
      
      
        
        
      • AI is the pattern-finder. You are the meaning-maker.
        • 
        
      • Skills never invent users, quotes, data or requirements. They ask, or mark an assumption.
        • 
        
      • Your own ideas and sketches come first. AI adds breadth and speed afterwards.
        • 
        
      • Anonymize research data before you paste it. Get consent before recording or using AI in a session.
        • 
        
      • Synthetic users are for rehearsal. They are never evidence.
        • 
        
      • One step at a time, with a saved checkpoint after each.
        • 
        
      • "Done!" from an AI is a claim. The check decides.
        • 
        
      • Log your prompts in PROMPTS.md.
        •

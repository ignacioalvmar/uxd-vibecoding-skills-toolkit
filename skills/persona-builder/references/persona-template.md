# Persona template

```
# [Name], [behavior-based descriptor]

Image suggestion: [neutral, situational, no stereotype cues]

> "[Quote from the data]" (P#)

## Bio
3 to 4 sentences, based only on the research data.

## Goals
- ... (3 to 4)

## Frustrations
- ... (3 to 4)

## Motivations
- ... (2 to 3)

## Behaviors
- What they do today, tools and workarounds they use

## Context of use
Where, when, on what device, under which constraints

## What we do not know yet
- Open questions the research did not answer

## Based on
Segment: [name], supported by [n] of [N] participants (P#, P#...)
```

## Proto-persona variant (no research data)

Use the same structure, with these changes:

- Title starts with "PROTO-PERSONA (assumptions, not research)".
- No quote.
- Every bullet is written as a hypothesis: "We assume that ...".
- Add a section "How we will test this" with the research needed to confirm or reject each assumption.

## Example: structured findings input

A good input for step 1 looks like this:

```
Product: SproutingBean, a mobile app for amateur gardeners.
Research goal: understand the challenges and motivations of novice gardeners.
Notes:
- P1: "I forget when I last watered my plants. I wish I had a simple log."
- P2: "I bought a plant from a store but have no idea how much sun it needs. I'm scared I'll kill it."
- P1: "I don't have a lot of space, so I need to know which plants will be happy on my windowsill."
- P3: "It feels like a real achievement when a new leaf grows."
```
